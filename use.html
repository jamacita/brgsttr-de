Use & boundaries

brgsttr.de is intentionally limited in scope.

It provides a stable contact and context point for selective exchange. It does not function as a public archive, portfolio, newsletter, social profile or general submission platform.

Appropriate use

Contact may be appropriate where a matter benefits from careful framing, controlled circulation, precise wording, structured assessment or discreet follow-up.

Not intended for

This site is not designed for unsolicited mass outreach, automated scraping, redistribution of materials, commercial reuse without permission or public reinterpretation of selectively shared content.

Materials and permissions

Selected materials may be shared directly and are not necessarily publicly listed. Commercial use, redistribution or derivative publication requires prior written permission.

Communication

Requests should include subject, purpose, relevant context and intended use. Concise messages are preferred.

Public surface

The limited public surface is intentional. Absence of a public listing does not imply absence of material, context or prior work.
