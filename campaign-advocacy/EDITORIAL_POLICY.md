# Campaign Advocacy — editorial maintenance policy

## 1. Purpose

This directory supports the public BRGSTTR campaign-advocacy reference layer. It documents specific external measures, initiatives, decisions or practices that are considered editorially notable in their historical and operational context.

## 2. Separation of fact and assessment

Each case must distinguish:

1. factual description supported by public sources; and
2. observed significance, which is an editorial interpretation.

The latter must not be presented as an objective certification or as a comprehensive assessment of an organisation.

## 3. Scale neutrality

Budget, organisational size, programme duration and rollout scale are not inclusion thresholds. A micro-measure, feature or pilot may qualify where its contextual significance is sufficiently clear.

## 4. Source threshold

Publication requires at least one sufficiently reliable public source. Primary sources are preferred. Secondary sources may supplement them where historical or contextual verification requires it.

## 5. No inferred relationship

Publication must not imply authorship, participation, commissioning, advisory involvement, sponsorship, partnership, client status or endorsement by the referenced entity unless such a relationship is separately public and intentionally disclosed.

## 6. No unpublished candidate registry in this repository

This repository is public. Unreviewed candidates, private observations, confidential background, personal contact history and internal deliberation must not be stored here.

Only cases approved for public treatment, or public-safe maintenance metadata about already published cases, belong in this directory.

## 7. Review states

- **pilot** — limited implementation under evaluation
- **active** — currently operating or maintained
- **scaled** — materially rolled out beyond initial implementation
- **completed** — programme concluded as planned
- **historical** — retained as a past reference
- **revised** — materially changed from the originally documented form

## 8. Withdrawal and revision

A case may be revised or withdrawn where:

- the factual basis changes;
- the measure is materially misdescribed;
- later evidence changes the context;
- the public source becomes unreliable or unavailable; or
- continued publication would create a materially misleading impression.

Historical value may remain relevant even where a measure is no longer current. The page should state that distinction explicitly.

## 9. Ordering

Cases are not ranked. Chronological, alphabetical or thematic ordering may be used for navigation only.
