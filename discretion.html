On discretion

Discretion is not opacity.

It is the disciplined handling of timing, context, exposure and access.

What discretion protects

Early thoughts, sensitive context and unfinished assessments should not be forced into public visibility too early.

How exchange begins

Contact should start with a clear subject, purpose and relevant context.

What to expect

A careful reading, proportionate response and no unnecessary circulation.
