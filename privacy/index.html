privacy.

This website is intentionally minimal. It does not use analytics, tracking pixels, cookies or embedded third-party content. The contact page includes a local email helper form that runs in your browser and opens your email app; it does not submit data to brgsttr.de.

Controller / contact

For privacy-related inquiries: welcome@brgsttr.de

Hosting

brgsttr.de is delivered via GitHub Pages. When a website is accessed, technical connection data such as IP address, time of access and requested resource can be processed in server logs for delivery and security.

Cookies / analytics

  • No cookies are set by this site.
  • No analytics or advertising tracking is used.
  • No newsletter, comments or server-side public submission forms are provided.

Contact via email and local helper form

If you send an email to welcome@brgsttr.de, the content of your message and your contact details are processed for handling the inquiry and any follow-up communication. The contact helper form on this site prepares such an email locally in your browser; data is only transmitted if you review and send the email through your own email application. Processing then occurs via the involved email providers.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights such as access, rectification, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability. Requests can be sent to welcome@brgsttr.de.
