boundaries.

brgsttr.de is intentionally limited in scope.

It provides a stable contact and context point for matters that benefit from discretion, clarity and careful handling. It is not intended as a public portfolio, newsletter or open archive.

Appropriate use

contact. may be appropriate where a matter benefits from careful framing, controlled circulation, precise wording, structured assessment or discreet follow-up.

Not intended for

This site is not intended for unsolicited mass outreach, automated scraping, redistribution of materials, commercial reuse without permission or public reinterpretation of selectively shared content.

Materials and permissions

Some materials may be shared directly when context and intended use are clear. Commercial use, redistribution or derivative publication requires prior written permission.

Communication

Requests should include subject, purpose, relevant context and intended use. Concise messages are preferred.

Public surface

The limited public surface is intentional. A missing public listing does not imply absence of material, context or prior work.
