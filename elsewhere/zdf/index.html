Public media · participation · legitimacy

From audience to legitimacy partner.

Public-service media legitimacy is not created solely through content quality, but also through transparency, institutional communication culture and meaningful audience participation.

Observations regarding public-service media, organisational transparency and participatory communication culture — based on public contribution formats such as ZDFmitreden.

Key observations

  1. Public-service audiences should not be understood merely as passive viewers, but as part of the institution’s broader legitimacy structure.
  2. Greater visibility of organisational value creation — including editorial, technical, operational and administrative functions — can strengthen institutional understanding and acceptance.
  3. Communication culture inside an organisation influences the credibility of communication directed outward toward the public.
  4. Institutional mission statements become more credible when strategic goals are formulated with operational clarity instead of abstract or overly generic language.
  5. Participation formats should evolve beyond symbolic feedback mechanisms toward more structurally integrated audience dialogue.

Context note

Derived from a public participation contribution submitted via ZDFmitreden in April 2025.
