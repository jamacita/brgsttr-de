Submission context

Materials may be shared for review, orientation or discussion.

Submission does not imply permission for commercial use, redistribution or derivative publication unless explicitly stated in writing.

Purpose

A submission may provide context, a structured observation, a proposal, a policy note or another form of controlled input.

Handling

Recipients are asked to treat submitted materials with proportionality, avoid unnecessary circulation and preserve the intended context.

Permissions

Commercial use, redistribution, publication or derivative use requires prior written permission unless the material itself states a different permission basis.

Follow-up

Where follow-up is appropriate, concise contact with subject, purpose and relevant context is preferred.
