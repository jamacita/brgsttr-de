Adjacent fields

Other contexts

A quiet note on adjacent fields of observation.

Current version: 14 May 2026. This version is preliminary and may be refined.

Purpose

This page outlines selected adjacent fields of observation beyond the primary contexts of tourism, hospitality, mobility, media, public policy and government relations.

It is intended as a low-noise orientation note. It does not represent a client list, partnership list, endorsement list or claim of formal engagement with any specific brand, company or public body.

Underlying principle

Similar patterns can appear across different environments: operational clarity, service quality, user perception, spatial logic, communication, trust, cleanliness, packaging, access, wayfinding and everyday usability.

The relevant question is not whether a field belongs to a specific industry category, but whether a situation contains conditions that can be observed, interpreted and translated into practical strategic value.

Adjacent fields

  • Retail and local service environments.
  • Food, quick service and everyday hospitality.
  • Consumer technology and app-based services.
  • Energy, home systems and installation logic.
  • Building, repair and maintenance contexts.
  • Product interfaces, packaging and handover situations.
  • Infrastructure, signage and wayfinding outside formal mobility projects.
  • Service operations where cleanliness, trust and perceived reliability matter.

What this is not

  • Not a client list.
  • Not a reference list.
  • Not a partnership statement.
  • Not an endorsement claim.
  • Not a public archive of case material.

In short

Other contexts are noted only where structured observation may help clarify how systems, interfaces, services or everyday environments create or reduce value.
